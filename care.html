UNDERSTAND. DON’T SELF-DIAGNOSE.

A question about your health?
Start with clarity.

Food, movement and rest can support your health. Symptoms and test results need the right professional assessment. This guide does not diagnose you or prescribe a diet.

“I feel tired most days.”

Persistent tiredness has many possible causes, including sleep, stress, medicines, anaemia or thyroid problems. A meal cannot identify the cause.

A useful next step

If it is unexplained, lasts for weeks or affects daily life, speak with a clinician. They may choose appropriate tests after assessment.

NHS: tiredness and fatigue ↗
“The skin on my neck has changed.”

New thickened, velvety patches can have several causes, including insulin resistance. Normal skin colour is not a disease, and appearance alone cannot diagnose diabetes.

A useful next step

Have a new or changing patch assessed by a clinician or dermatologist. This site does not analyse skin photographs or promise a change in skin colour.

American Academy of Dermatology ↗
Glucose, insulin and muscle

Insulin is produced by the pancreas. Insulin resistance means cells in muscle, fat and liver do not respond well to it. Activity can improve insulin sensitivity; that is not the same as repairing the pancreas.

A useful next step

Marked thirst, frequent urination or unexplained weight loss deserve medical assessment. Some people have no symptoms. Tests, not a website score, help clinicians assess glucose regulation.

NIDDK: insulin resistance and prediabetes ↗
“My report mentions fatty liver.”

Fatty liver can be silent. Clinical assessment may include history, blood tests and imaging; liver enzymes alone do not tell the whole story. Food and activity can support the agreed care plan.

A useful next step

Ask the treating clinician what the finding means and whether fibrosis assessment or follow-up is needed. Roza does not promise liver reversal, detoxification or a fixed result.

NIDDK: fatty liver diagnosis ↗
“I am sleepy even after a night in bed.”

Loud snoring, gasping or pauses in breathing alongside daytime sleepiness deserve attention. More sleep time alone may not address the cause.

A useful next step

Discuss these signs with a healthcare professional; a sleep study may be appropriate. Food delivery does not treat sleep apnoea.

NHLBI: sleep apnoea symptoms ↗
What does BMI actually tell me?

BMI relates weight to height. It does not distinguish muscle from fat or diagnose metabolic health. South Asian people can have cardiometabolic risk at a lower BMI.

A useful next step

Use it as one limited screening measure with professional interpretation. Our body calculation is optional and withholds estimates in review-first situations.

NICE: assessment and BMI ↗

UNDERSTANDING TESTS

Know what the test can tell you.

A clinician chooses the right tests and interprets the results. There is no universal Roza test package.

HbA1c

Reflects average blood glucose over roughly three months. Some conditions affect accuracy; confirmation may be needed.

Glucose tests

Fasting plasma glucose or an oral glucose tolerance test may be used when indicated. A home reading is not the same as a confirmed diagnosis.

Liver assessment

Blood tests and imaging answer different questions. Your clinician decides whether further fibrosis assessment is needed.

FOOD + MOVEMENT + REST

A routine has more than one part.

Carbohydrates, protein and fat all have a place. Our proposed service focuses on familiar meals and thoughtful portions, not a claim that keto or fasting is wrong for everyone.

General adult activity guidance includes 150–300 minutes of moderate activity a week and muscle-strengthening on at least two days. Build gradually and adapt to your ability and clinical advice. No exercise is required to “earn” a meal.

If you take insulin or certain diabetes medicines, delayed meals, fasting or increased activity can cause low glucose. Discuss changes with your care team. Do not change medicines through this website.

ADA: individualised nutrition · WHO: activity · NIDDK: low glucose

Practical support starts with your day.
